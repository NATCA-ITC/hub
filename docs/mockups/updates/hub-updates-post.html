National 🚨 Urgent

FAA Shutdown Contingency Plan — Action Required

ND
Nick Daniels
NATCA President
📅 April 10, 2026 · 2:14 PM ET ⏱️ 4 min read

Brothers and sisters, as you're aware, Congress remains in active negotiation on the FY2026 continuing resolution. With the current CR set to expire at midnight Friday, NATCA leadership is coordinating directly with FAA Administrator Washington on contingency operations should a shutdown occur.

Immediate Action Items

All facility representatives must complete the following within the next 48 hours:

  • Review the attached FAA/NATCA Joint Shutdown Contingency Memorandum
  • Schedule a local briefing with your membership — templates available in the Resources section below
  • Update your facility's emergency contact tree in the Hub Members directory
  • Identify any members who have outstanding leave requests that may be affected
"This situation remains fluid. We are in constant communication with the Administrator and with Congressional leadership on both sides of the aisle. Our priority is ensuring every member understands their rights and responsibilities in the event of a lapse in appropriations."

What We Know

Based on the most recent conversations with FAA leadership, air traffic operations will continue under excepted status as they have in previous shutdowns. However, support functions — training, administrative, and certain safety programs — will be suspended. Key facts:

  • Controllers in operational positions must report to work as scheduled
  • Pay will be retroactively provided once a funding bill is enacted
  • Training classes at the FAA Academy will pause on Day 1 of any shutdown
  • NATCA Member Assistance Program (MAP) will remain fully funded and operational

Resources

  • FAA/NATCA Joint Contingency Memorandum (PDF)
  • Facility Briefing Template — Shutdown 2026
  • Financial Assistance Resources for NATCA Members
  • Legal Rights During a Government Shutdown (Gary Peters, General Counsel)

I will continue to provide updates here and through your Regional Vice Presidents as the situation develops. Stay informed, stay united, and take care of each other.

In solidarity,
Nick Daniels
President, NATCA
