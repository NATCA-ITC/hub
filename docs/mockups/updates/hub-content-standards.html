Content Standards

Content Standards

How Hub organizes, classifies, and connects content across platforms. Every piece of NATCA communication — from a breaking legislative alert to a reference SOP — follows this taxonomy so members can find it now and 2 years from now.

The Three Axes

Every Hub story is classified on three independent axes. These are not interchangeable — each answers a different question. Authors set all three when publishing; the system uses them for display, filtering, Discord routing, and email delivery.

Axis Question it answers Values Where it's used
Content Type What kind of thing is this? Document, Coverage, Action Needed, Advisory, Announcement, Event Discord tags, Hub card styling, feed filters
Topic What area does this belong to? Pay, Benefits, Working Conditions, Legislative, Safety Discord channel routing, Hub topic filter chips, email subscription lists
Lifecycle Where is this in its life? Developing, Active, Action Needed, Resolved, Reference Discord tags, Hub status badges, auto-archive rules
Content Types

Axis 1 — what kind of thing is this? The content type determines how the story is displayed in Hub and how it's tagged in Discord. Each story gets exactly one content type.

TypeWhen to useExampleHub display
📋 Document SOPs, MOUs, contract interpretations, official policy Long-Term Hold SOP, Slate Book Article 35 MOU Document card with "Related Documents" linking to DMS
📰 Coverage Rolling, multi-update stories — the liveblog FAA Reauthorization, Controller Staffing Crisis Story with update timeline, "Developing" indicator
⚡ Action Needed Members must do something by a date NIW registration deadline, vote on tentative agreement Action card with deadline countdown, CTA button
ℹ️ Advisory Awareness — no action required, not rolling ASRS procedure changes, new ERAM version deploying Info card, neutral styling
📢 Announcement One-time communication, no expected follow-up Locality pay tables released, election results Simple card, no update timeline
📅 Event Has start/end dates CFS Conference, NATCA in Washington, regional training Event card with date range, registration link

Lifecycle

Axis 3 — where is this in its life? Not all content goes through every stage. An SOP goes straight to Reference. A shutdown story goes Developing → Resolved. The lifecycle determines the Hub status badge and Discord tag.

🔄

→
🟢
Active

→
✅

Done, outcome known
⚡

Evergreen — consult as needed

Key distinction: Reference ≠ Resolved. An MOU is Reference from the moment it's signed — it was never Developing. A shutdown story goes Developing → Resolved, and its associated SOPs remain Reference. These are different paths.

The Bridge: Hub ↔ DMS

Hub explains and discusses. DMS stores and governs. They link to each other — neither contains the other. Every path leads to both the context (Hub story) and the artifact (DMS document).

📰 Hub Story

The communication and context layer:

  • What changed and why it matters
  • Timeline of updates
  • Member discussion

📎 DMS Document

The canonical artifact:

  • The actual SOP, MOU, or policy PDF
  • Versioned, has owner + review date
  • Citeable in grievances
  • Searchable in DMS

Finding things 18 months later

Search pathResult

Cross-Platform Flow

Hub is the canonical source. Discord and email are distribution channels. DMS is the document archive. Here's how they connect.

PlatformRoleWhat lives hereLinks to
Hub Canonical source Stories + updates, Tiptap-authored, permanent URL DMS (related docs), Discord (forum post), Email (campaign)
Discord Discussion + notification Forum posts (by topic), announcement feed, member threads Hub (via buttons on every post)
DMS Document archive Official PDFs — SOPs, MOUs, contract interpretations Hub (related stories, computed)
Email Push delivery Campaigns per topic subscription, digests, alerts Hub (every email links to the Hub story)
